# Client Brief

Saved at: 2026-08-15

Dashboard project: 1982 Topps Donkey Kong Archive

## Client Name

GoldenPinocchio

## Business Name

1982 Topps Donkey Kong Archive

## Business Summary

An unofficial historical collector archive for the 1982 Topps Donkey Kong trading cards, built around high-resolution card scans and arcade-era presentation.

## Website Goal

Create a memorable collector/archive site that feels like walking into a 1982 Donkey Kong arcade display.

## Primary Cta

Enter the Arcade

## Target Audience

Collectors, retro gaming fans, Donkey Kong fans, and trading-card archive visitors.

## Services

Collection viewing, puzzle exploration, scratch-off card display, card checklist tracking, history timeline.

## Tone Voice

Nostalgic, playful, archival, arcade-like, and museum-quality.

## Visual Style

1981–1982 arcade machine, CRT scanlines, pixel art, Nintendo red/brown/blue/gold palette, subtle glow and flicker.

## Pages Needed

Home, The Cards, The Puzzles, Scratch-Offs, The Pack, History, Checklist

## Must Include

- 32 sticker cards
- 4 scratch-off/game cards
- puzzle backs reference
- custom cursor idea
- interactive card viewer
- collector checklist
- unofficial archive disclaimer

## Avoid

Modern corporate styling, plain database layout, official Nintendo branding claims, dull checkbox-only presentation.

## Brand Assets

High-resolution scans from MarioWiki category page.

## Inspiration Links

- https://www.interactivegallery.co/

## Extra Notes

The site should feel like an interactive digital museum built inside a 1982 arcade machine.
