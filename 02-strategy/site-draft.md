# 1982 Topps Donkey Kong Archive — Draft Direction

Saved at: 2026-08-15

## Working Title

**1982 Topps Donkey Kong Archive**

## One-Line Concept

An unofficial historical collector archive that feels like stepping into a 1982 Donkey Kong arcade cabinet filled with trading cards, CRT glow, and subtle interactive museum polish.

## North Star

**Simple and stunning.**

The site should feel:
- instantly understandable
- visually rich but not busy
- nostalgic without becoming noisy
- interactive without feeling like a game menu
- archival without feeling dry

## Core Audience

- Donkey Kong fans
- vintage trading card collectors
- arcade/Nintendo nostalgia visitors
- people who enjoy beautiful interactive archive sites

## Primary Goal

Make the card set feel special, collectible, and worth exploring.

## Tone

- nostalgic
- playful
- museum-like
- confident
- restrained
- premium

## Visual Direction

Think:
- black CRT cabinet glass
- deep navy and near-black background
- Nintendo red accents
- Donkey Kong brown tones
- coin-gold highlights
- tiny pops of arcade blue and CRT green
- scanlines, bloom, and soft glow used lightly

The look should be **clean first**, with arcade effects used as seasoning, not the whole meal.

## What Makes It Strong

1. One strong hero moment.
2. A clean card grid with great spacing.
3. Gorgeous card viewer.
4. Interactive puzzle display.
5. One memorable custom cursor.
6. Minimal nav and simple sections.

## Recommended Site Structure

- Home
- The Cards
- The Puzzles
- Scratch-Offs
- The Pack
- History
- Checklist

## Homepage Direction

The homepage should open like an arcade title screen and then settle into a calm gallery.

### Intro sequence copy

NINTENDO
PRESENTS

DONKEY KONG

TOPPS
1982

INSERT COIN

PRESS START

### Main hero copy

**TOPPS DONKEY KONG — 1982**

*The original Donkey Kong trading card experience.*

Button:

**Enter the Arcade**

### Hero layout idea

- centered title stack
- one featured card or packaging image floating in the center
- subtle slow rotation or parallax
- faint scanlines and CRT flicker
- one primary CTA only

## Homepage Sections

### 1. Hero
- title
- subtitle
- enter button
- featured scan or floating card

### 2. Quick Facts
A small row of clean stat tiles:
- 32 sticker cards
- 4 scratch-off/game cards
- 2 puzzle backs
- unofficial collector archive

### 3. Featured Cards
A few spotlight cards, not the full grid.

### 4. Explore the Set
Three or four large entry cards:
- The Cards
- The Puzzles
- Scratch-Offs
- History

### 5. Collector Note
A short disclaimer and archive note.

## Card Collection Page

This is the main browsing experience.

### Layout
- grid of all 32 sticker cards
- strong spacing
- each card feels like a physical object
- hover tilt, glow, and slight zoom
- click opens large viewer

### Card detail format
Example:

**#06 — JUMP!**
1982 Topps Donkey Kong
Sticker Card #06
Puzzle Position: Blue Puzzle — Bottom Left

### Viewer behavior
When clicked:
- open large scan
- show front and back if available
- card number, title, set info
- puzzle position if known
- next/previous navigation

## Puzzle Page

A playful but still elegant interactive section.

### Concept
Show the backs as two puzzles:
- Blue Puzzle
- Other Puzzle

### Interaction
- cards begin scattered or arranged loosely
- user can drag into positions
- completed puzzle reveals a quiet celebration

### Completion message

**LEVEL COMPLETE!**

**10,000 POINTS**

Keep the animation subtle and satisfying, not cartoonish.

## Scratch-Off Page

Treat this like an instruction panel inside an arcade cabinet.

Include:
- the 4 scratch-off/game cards
- front and back views
- short explanation of how they worked
- historical context

## The Pack Page

Show packaging, wrappers, or product context.

This page should feel like a clean display case.

## History Page

Keep it concise and visual.

### Timeline
- 1981 — Donkey Kong arcade release
- 1982 — Topps Donkey Kong cards
- 1982 — Scratch-off/game cards
- Today — collector archive

### Behavior
- mark cards collected
- play coin sound on completion
- update progress visually
- keep it easy to scan

## Cursor Concept

Use one simple custom cursor idea, not several competing ones.

Recommended default:
- tiny pixel-art Mario / Jumpman cursor

Optional hover state:
- pointing hand or hammer sprite

Optional click state:
- tiny jump animation

Keep it subtle so the site still feels elegant.

## Motion Rules

Use motion lightly:
- slow card drift
- gentle hover tilt
- soft glow on focus
- subtle CRT flicker
- tiny scanline animation
- minimal glitch moments only

Avoid:
- nonstop motion
- loud shake effects
- overdone particle spam
- too many competing animations

## Sound Direction

Optional and minimal:
- coin sound on major interactions
- tiny arcade blip on card open
- subtle level-complete tone for puzzle finish

Default should still work quietly.

## Copy Style

Keep all copy short and confident.

Good:
- Enter the Arcade
- View the Set
- Open Card Viewer
- Puzzle Mode
- Collector Archive

Avoid long paragraphs on the homepage.

## Trust / Disclaimer Copy

**1982 Topps Donkey Kong**
*An unofficial historical archive for collectors.*

This should be visible but understated.

## Design Guardrails

- simple layout
- limited nav
- no clutter
- no giant blocks of text
- no modern corporate styling
- no fake official Nintendo branding
- no bright toy-store palette
- no overdesigned dashboard feel

## Best First Build

Start with:
1. homepage
2. card grid
3. card viewer
4. history strip
5. checklist

Then add:
6. puzzles
7. scratch-offs
8. custom cursor
9. audio polish

## Final Feel

The best version of this site should feel like:

**a beautiful 1982 arcade machine turned into an interactive collector museum**
