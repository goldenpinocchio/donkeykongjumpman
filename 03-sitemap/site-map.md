- establish the arcade/CRT concept

- direct users into the archive

### The Cards
- full 32-card gallery
- card viewer experience
- collector browsing

### The Puzzles
- interactive puzzle backs display

- playful historical interaction

### Scratch-Offs
- 4 scratch-off/game cards
- front/back display
- short explanation

### The Pack
- packaging and product context
- display-case style presentation

### History
- compact timeline
- historical context without overload

### Checklist
- completion tracking
- high-score style progress view
- collector status

## Homepage Section Order

1. Intro / arcade title screen
2. Hero card / title
3. Quick facts
4. Featured cards
5. Entry cards to major sections
6. Archive disclaimer

## Primary CTA Placement

- Hero button: **Enter the Arcade**
- Secondary buttons: Explore Cards, View Puzzles, Open Checklist

## What Should Stay Simple

- one strong hero
- one main navigation row
- one featured image/card at a time
- one primary call to action
- limited text on the homepage

## Questions to Resolve Later

- whether sound is on by default or user-controlled
- whether the collection grid opens in modal or dedicated viewer page

## Approval Notes

This sitemap is intentionally lean. It is built to support a simple, beautiful first version without overcomplicating the site.
